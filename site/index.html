FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything renders on your Mac.

Is it open source?

Yes. The code is open source under the AGPL-3.0, at github.com/consolelabs/spacedown. Releases up to v1.1.0 stay MIT. A Pro upgrade is coming.

Is it free?

Yes. The reader is free, with no trial and no time limit.

Which macOS?

macOS 13 or later.
