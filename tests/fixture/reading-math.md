# Math under a reading mode

A rotation by $\theta$ followed by a rotation by $\phi$ is one rotation by $\theta + \phi$, so the
annuity factor $F = \frac{(1+r)^n - 1}{r}$ and the identity $R(\theta)R(\phi) = R(\theta+\phi)$ must
still typeset when a saved reading mode wraps the prose around them.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F &= \frac{1.08^{10} - 1}{0.08} && \text{[future value of an annuity]} \\
  &= 14.4866 && \text{[divide]}
\end{aligned}
$$
