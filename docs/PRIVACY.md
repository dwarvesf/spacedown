# Spacedown privacy policy

Spacedown collects no data.

- The Quick Look extension renders the file you preview on your Mac, in process. It makes no network requests; its content security policy blocks remote content.
- The app has no accounts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
- Nothing you preview leaves your Mac.

The optional Safari drop extension and the command-line tool, available when you build from source, also run locally. The command-line tool's browser output loads KaTeX from a public CDN to render math; no document content is sent.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dwarvesf/spacedown/issues.
